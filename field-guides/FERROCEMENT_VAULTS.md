## Physics & mathematics (lowest-node usable)
- Catenary = the curve a hanging chain makes under its own weight.
  Inversion of the catenary produces a pure-compression arch (no bending moment).
- Ferrocement = high-cement mortar + densely distributed small-diameter mesh.
  Acts as a thin shell. Compound curvature raises stiffness dramatically.
- Sacred geometry entry points: circle, vesica piscis, Seed of Life (7 circles),
  catenary, and the 7-fold completeness that appears in both structure and time.

## Catamaran-style / modular arches
No single free “catamaran ferrocement” manual exists.
Method that produces the same result:
- Build pairs of catenary ribs (wood or light steel temporary form)
- Cover with multiple layers of mesh
- Plaster both sides with high-cement mortar
- Remove form after cure
- Join parallel arches with longitudinal stringers or continuous vault
This is the classic Nervi / thin-shell method applied modularly.

## Practical sequence for one vault
1. Lay out catenary on ground with chain or string.
2. Build temporary rib forms.
3. Tie multiple layers of mesh (chicken wire + small rebar if available).
4. Plaster in thin layers, both faces.
5. Cure 7–21 days (keep moist).
6. Strip forms. Structure stands in pure compression.
