# Black Inner Core + Vacuum Chamber → Temperature Differential

## Mechanism
1. Black surface (α ≈ 0.95–1.0) absorbs nearly all incident radiation (solar, IR, or internal).
2. Vacuum jacket eliminates convective and conductive heat transfer.
3. Outer shell can be reflective or selectively emissive.
4. Result: large ΔT between core and outer environment with minimal parasitic loss.

## Simple model (Stefan-Boltzmann)
Power absorbed by black core ≈ α · A · σ · T_rad⁴
Power radiated by core ≈ ε · A · σ · T_core⁴
In steady state under vacuum the temperature rise is limited mainly by radiation and any residual conduction through supports.

Practical effect:
- Passive solar concentration or waste-heat capture into the core.
- Outer shell stays near ambient while core runs hot (or vice-versa with cooling designs).
- Usable for small heat engines, thermoelectric, or orientation-stable thermal mass inside Cloud9 tensegrity platforms.
